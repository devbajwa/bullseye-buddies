# Bullseye Buddies

A bright archery game for kids that runs in the browser. Play it at **https://bullseye-buddies.netlify.app/**

Built by Waqas Bajwa with AI-assisted development. I designed the game, played and tested every build myself, and had the AI fix the issues I found, such as character movement, gameplay and performance.

## How to play

- Move the mouse, or your finger, to aim the crosshair.
- Hold down to pull the bow back. Let go when the ring around the crosshair turns green. Space works too.
- Watch the flag and the wind meter. Wind pushes the arrow, so wait for a calm moment or aim into it.
- The gold centre is worth 10 points. Score enough for up to three stars and unlock the next level.
- Pop balloons for 5 bonus points.

## What is in it

- Three bows (Oak, Swift and Steady) and three worlds: Sunny Meadow, Sandy Beach and Snowy Peaks.
- Wind that changes during play, shown by a flag and a meter.
- Music and sound effects made with the Web Audio API, each with its own on and off switch.
- Fullscreen mode. Progress is saved in the browser.

## Files

The whole game is `index.html`: markup, styles and script, drawn on a canvas, with no dependencies and nothing to build. Open it in a browser to play.
