How to play

  • 🖱️Move your mouse (or finger) to aim the crosshair.
  • ✊Hold down to pull the bow back. The ring around the crosshair fills up. Wait till it turns green!
  • 🏹Let go to shoot. You can also hold and release Space.
  • 🚩Watch the flag and the wind meter. Wind pushes your arrow! Wait for a calm moment, or aim a little the other way.
  • ⭐The middle (gold) is 10 points. Score points to earn up to 3 stars and unlock the next level.
  • 🎈Pop balloons for +5 bonus points!
